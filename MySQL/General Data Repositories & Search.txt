General Data Repositories & Search Engines
These platforms offer a wide variety of datasets across many domains, making them excellent starting points for any project.
● Google Dataset Search: A powerful search engine specifically for datasets.
○ Link: https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/
● Kaggle: A highly popular platform for data science, featuring numerous datasets, competitions, and community-contributed notebooks.
○ Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets
● Data.gov:
● The official portal for open data from the U.S. government.
○ Link: https://www.data.gov/
● Data.world:
● A collaborative data platform with a vast collection of user-uploaded datasets.
○ Link: https://data.world/
● UCI Machine Learning Repository:
● A classic and well-established source for machine learning datasets.
○ Link: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/datasets
● DataHub: Offers a comprehensive collection of free and open datasets across various topics.
○ Link: https://datahub.io/
● GitHub (Awesome-Public-Datasets): Curated lists of public datasets on GitHub. Search for repositories like "awesome-public-datasets".
○ Link (example): https://github.com/awesomedata/awesome-public-datasets
● AWS Data Exchange: A platform offering various datasets from different providers, including free public datasets.
○ Link: https://aws.amazon.com/data-exchange/
● OpenML: Focused on machine learning datasets, often used for benchmarking algorithms.
○ Link: https://www.openml.org/
